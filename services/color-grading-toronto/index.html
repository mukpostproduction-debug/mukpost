// PRODUCER & POST SUPERVISOR FAQ

Turnover Specs, Camera Formats & Turnaround.

What camera formats does Muk Post grade in DaVinci Resolve Studio?

Muk Post grades all major cinema and broadcast formats natively in DaVinci Resolve Studio, including ARRI Alexa (ARRIRAW / ProRes LogC3 & LogC4), RED EPIC-W 8K and V-Raptor (REDCODE RAW R3D), Sony Venice / FX6 / FX3 (X-OCN & S-Log3), Blackmagic URSA Mini Pro (BRAW), and 16mm Kodak / Super 8 motion picture film scans.

How do we prep our timeline in Premiere Pro, Final Cut, or Resolve for color grading?

Export an XML or AAF alongside a reference H.264/ProRes offline cut with burned-in timecode, plus trimmed camera raw/log media (with 24–48 frame handles) or full camera cards. If you edit in DaVinci Resolve, you can send a Project Archive (.dra) or use our Ultimate DaVinci Resolve Studio Template for instant zero-conform turnover.

Can we attend color grading sessions in Toronto or review remotely?

Yes. We support local drive drop-offs and attended reviews in Toronto (GTA), as well as real-time remote grading sessions and pixel-accurate 4K Frame.io review links for producers in Toronto, London, NYC, LA, and worldwide.
