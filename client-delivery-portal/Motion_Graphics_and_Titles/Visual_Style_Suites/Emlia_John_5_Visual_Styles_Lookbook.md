## 2. Style Suite Details

### Style 01: Classic Architectural Heritage (The Den 1880 Look)
- **Opening Title:** `E M L I A   &   J O H N` in Cormorant Garamond with 1.35x tracking; sub: `SEPTEMBER 26, 2026 // DEN 1880, WATERLOO`.
- **Speaker Lower Third:** Solid 1.5px antique gold hairline rule separating speaker name from family relationship.
- **Motion Behavior:** 24-frame slow optical dissolve (Gaussian 10px &rarr; 0px) with 102% subtle digital scale drift.
- **Ideal Fit:** Couples who love classic wedding elegance, historic architecture, and timeless romance.

### Style 02: Modern Editorial Minimalist (Vogue / Kinfolk Style)
- **Opening Title:** `EMLIA & JOHN` in Syne Bold with generous letter spacing; sub: `26 . 09 . 2026 — WATERLOO, ON`.
- **Speaker Lower Third:** 2px solid pure white rule with bold all-caps name in lower left 10% title-safe margin.
- **Motion Behavior:** Crisp 12-frame opacity cuts or bottom-up mask reveal with zero motion blur.
- **Ideal Fit:** Couples with modern artistic sensibilities who want their wedding film to feel like high-end fashion cinema.

### Style 03: Romantic Nostalgic Super-8 (1970s Kodachrome Warmth)
- **Typography:** Playfair Display italic/regular paired with Courier Prime typewriter metadata.
- **Styling Accent:** 2px dashed amber rule (`#E29D52`) with buttermilk white text.
- **Optical Pairing:** Blends perfectly with `Film_Gate_35mm_Overlay_4K.png` and warm tungsten lens flares.
- **Ideal Fit:** Intimate, emotional wedding films with a warm, nostalgic heartbeat.

### Style 04: Understated Arthouse Luxury (A24 / Cinematheque Minimal)
- **Typography:** Inter Light with extreme letter spacing (+0.45em) paired with subtle Space Mono coordinates.
- **Styling Accent:** 1px whisper-thin mineral sage hairline (`#9EAAA5`).
- **Motion Behavior:** 30-frame ultra-slow Gaussian dissolve from soft focus to crisp lines.
- **Ideal Fit:** Poetic, dialogue-driven highlight films that rely on subtle emotional glances.

### Style 05: Contemporary Botanical Warmth (Courtyard Linen & Earthen)
- **Typography & Palette:** Sculpted Cinzel serif in soft linen white (`#FAF7F2`) paired with earthy terracotta rules (`#B89578`).
- **Symmetry & Layout:** Centered classical alignment matching bridal floral arrangements and courtyard light.
- **Ideal Fit:** Outdoor/courtyard ceremonies and romantic autumn weddings.

## 3. How to Test & Switch Styles in DaVinci Resolve
1. Navigate to:  
   `06_Exports/Client_Delivery_Portal/Motion_Graphics_and_Titles/Visual_Style_Suites/`
2. Open any style folder (e.g., `01_Classic_Architectural_Heritage/`).
3. Drag the `.setting` macro directly into DaVinci Resolve onto Video Track 3 above your footage.
4. All fonts, tracking, colors, and optical keyframes populate automatically!
